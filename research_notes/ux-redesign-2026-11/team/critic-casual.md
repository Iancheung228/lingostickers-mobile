# Casual moment-capturer critique (French before a trip, loves journaling apps and BeReal)

## First 30 seconds
I open the app and `baseline/01a-signed-out-sign-in-intro.png` is a huge empty cream screen with a login box at the bottom. It says "WELCOME BACK" to someone who has never been here, and there is no sticker, photo or demo. It could be any app. I'd bounce unless a friend sent me.

If I get past that, the populated Home (`ss6-home.png`) is lovely at the top: the pile of cutout stickers (cup, croissant, cat) is the whole pitch. Then it turns into a pile of surfaces. There is a "Latest finds" row, a "Latest stickers" list, a filter row and then a grid, all showing the same 7 things. The floating pill nav sits on top of content and clips it (`ss6-home.png`, `ss4-collection.png`). The header has "GOAL 5", a search icon, a gear and "7 due 7 min" at once. I don't know what a "goal" is, and "7 due" reads as a debt.

The fresh account (`02a-home-top.png`) is worse. My first sticker is a blue placeholder "A" icon with a tape label saying "new", and the top says "0 FOUND TODAY". Nothing tells me to take a photo.

Cheap or confusing:
- `ss1-card.png` and `ss3-review.png`: the card is a wall of tiny grey spaced-out mono caps (SAY IT, MEANS · NOUN, IN USE, WORD BY WORD), plus a grammar tip and "Hold the mic to record your pronunciation". I wanted my croissant and one word. This is a textbook page.
- `06a-scan-first-view.png`: my first tap on the camera tab is a legal wall naming Google, Replicate and remove.bg. I understand why, but the first moment of the main feature is a consent form, not a camera.
- `06h-scan-result-server-error.png`: "Couldn't find the word. Something went wrong on our side." is a dead end, and the Box/Lasso toggle shows dark-on-dark text.
- `07a-boards-empty.png`: two "new board" buttons, a gear floating on top of the clock, and an empty void. Boards doesn't explain why I'd care.
- `08a-profile-top.png`: "Welcome back to your cozy corner! You've captured 1 sticker so far. Keep it up! 🌸" is the vibe-coded part.

## The show-a-friend moment
Two things. First, `ss4-collection.png` and the Home sticker pile: my coffee, my bike and a cat cut out and labelled "La tasse / The Cup". That is the screenshot I'd post. Second, `ss2-scene.png`: one photo of a breakfast table gives me several words (verser, boire, déjeuner) and I choose which to keep. That is the magic and it is buried behind a scroll and tiny chips.

I'd want to share a finished sticker, one photo with the word on it, as one image. The card has share and download icons, but they are tiny icon-only buttons in a toolbar.

## Coming back tomorrow without a guilt-trip
- A "today" prompt like BeReal: one nudge, "What's on your desk today?", and the camera opens. Not "7 due".
- A gentle "on this day" or "one from last week" that shows a photo and asks me to say the word. That is review wearing a memory costume.
- No red. `ss3-review.png` has Again in alarm red, and a "0 reviewed" counter in the calendar. A red button and a "7 due in 7 days" header feel like a bill. Say "7 stickers want a hello".
- Streaks only if they're soft (a gold bar, not a lost streak).

## What feels like homework
- Four grade buttons with times (Again 1m / Hard 6m / Good 10m / Easy 4d). I don't know what "Hard 6m" means. Two buttons, "Got it" and "Not yet", would do.
- The review front is a naked sticker with a "What did you call this?" prompt and a Hint button, which is okay (`04a-study-front.png`). But the back is the whole textbook again.
- "Date added – newest" sort, favourites filter, categories (Kitchen, Animal) and the search icon. That is a spreadsheet.
- Phonetics like [luh krwah-sahn] in monospace. Useful, but it looks like code.

## Calendar and Boards: my memories
Calendar (`05a-calendar-month.png`, `ss5-calendar.png`) is nearly right: a month grid where days show the sticker I found. Make it a photo diary. Days should show the sticker big, and tapping a day should open "Thursday: cup, croissant, bike", with a line I can type ("the café by the canal"). The Day screen (`05b-calendar-day-with-card.png`) already has "Add what happened that day...", but that field is hidden in a dashed box. Cut the weekly bar chart ("1 found / 0 reviewed") and the "The long view" title, which are admin. Put the "Finds / Reviews" switch somewhere quieter.

Boards should be my scrapbook: a trip board, a "Paris" wall, a friend's dog wall, with stickers I drag around and share as a picture. The empty state says "A board is a wall you arrange yourself" but gives me nothing to start with. Offer a ready board such as "My first week", pre-filled with my stickers.

## Rose vs Powder
Powder. The Rose screens (`ss4-collection.png`, `ss5-calendar.png`) are a heavy salmon header, a pink pill nav and maroon text, and they feel like 2019 and slightly sad. My stickers are food and animals in saturated colours, and they sit better on cool white and light blue, where they pop, than on pink-beige, where the croissant and the cat sink into the page. The peach action colour in Powder also feels friendly rather than serious. One caution: the baseline art is already blue (the Expo A placeholder), so I'm only judging the stickers.

## Where I'd disagree with an Anki power user
- They want four grades and interval previews. I want two, and I'd never open a settings page to find out why.
- They want dense cards and fast review in a list. I want one big sticker and one word, with the grammar behind a tap.
- They want a deck browser, filters, sort and search first. I want the camera first and a photo-diary feel, and the library second.
- They'd call "on this day" and soft nudges gimmicks. I'd call them the reason I open the app at all.
- We'd agree on one thing: it must be honest, and the scheduler should still run underneath without me knowing.
