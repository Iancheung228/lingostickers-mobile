# Casual critic, round 2 (rebuttal)

I read all seven round-1 files. Where I move, I say so.

**1. Colour: Powder, and Easy stays green.** I still vote Powder. The learning role says peach sits too close to Again/Hard, but the grade row lives on the study back, where brand colour is already absent. Accessibility's navy ink #1B3359 plus a ring answers the 1.98:1 fill problem. Peach is the colour of my croissant and my sticker pile, so it should be the one action colour. A blue Easy (accessibility) would be the third blue on Powder, next to the navy chrome and the pale-blue surfaces, and I would read it as "selected" instead of "easy". I'd keep green and make colour-blind safety do its work with a check glyph and the interval text. Accessibility's own point is that labels already carry the meaning. Fine on Rose either way.

**2. Grading: I move.** I asked for two buttons. After reading serious and learning, I accept four buttons where Again and Good are the large, drawn pair and Hard and Easy are smaller and secondary. This is the learning role's lesson #9 rule that the rarer action gets the quieter control. Swipe (interaction) should be opt-in in Profile, off by default. My real ask is copy: below the interval, show a plain word ("Again", "Good") but never "Hard 6m" in loud type. Undo is good.

**3. Home order, top to bottom.** I give ground to serious and learning, and the designer's single Due card:
1. Title plus avatar.
2. One compact Due card: "7 stickers want a hello", a peach button, no red and no debt wording. When nothing is due, it becomes "Take today's photo" and opens the camera.
3. A "From this week" or "On this day" strip, one big sticker with its caption. This is my one non-negotiable. It is the memory, and it is review wearing a costume.
4. The grid.
I disagree with the serious critic about the animated sticker wall. I'd keep it only in Boards, not Home, and that is the one thing I concede. Cut "GOAL 5" and the duplicate lists.

**4. Calendar.** One screen cannot do both well. I side with the learning role on the forecast, but move it: the "7 due in 7 days" bar chart goes to Profile stats or the Due card. Calendar becomes the diary: a big sticker per day, "On this day", and a typed line. That line is the same note the learning role wants on the study front, so it's a single feature.

**5. Navigation.** No objection to a labelled full-width bar with a raised Scan, and the Scan modal is right because the camera should feel immediate. An avatar in a shared header is better than six gears. I'd keep the consent page out of the first tap: ask once at the first photo, inline.

**6. Study card.** I accept learning's point: no share, delete or avatar mid-review. The memory goes where it costs nothing: a one-line note and the date as muted text under the photo on the front, and the full detail (when, where, story) one tap away on the back. The memory is the photo plus my note, and that is enough. I'd drop "Not recorded" entirely.

**One line: the single change that matters most** is to make the first screen after sign-in a calm "your photo, your word" moment (one Due card, one big memory sticker, one obvious camera) instead of four lists of the same thing.
