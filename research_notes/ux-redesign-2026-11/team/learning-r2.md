# Learning: round 2 rebuttal

**1. Colour: I change my vote to Powder, on one condition.** Critic-serious is right that cool chrome makes the red/green ramp unmistakable, and my round-1 worry (peach near Again/Hard) is solvable: the grade row never uses the brand colour (lesson #12), and peach is only the primary CTA ("Start", "Add cards", "Done"). The condition: the grade tokens are their own set, not derived from the palette. **Blue Easy: reject.** Under Powder it is the same hue as the chrome (#24406B navy, #E4F0FB tint), so Easy would read as "neutral/selected", the opposite of "best". Under Rose it is merely off-system. Accessibility's real concern is red/green CVD; fix it by lightness and shape, not hue: Again = filled dark brick, Hard = sand outline, Good = pale green tint, Easy = filled deep green, plus the interval text on every button and fixed left-to-right order. Verify with a deuteranopia simulation in the contrast script.

**2. Grading: one design.** Four buttons stay, but Again and Good are primary (wider, ~38% each), Hard and Easy secondary (~12% each, lower-contrast outline, still labelled with intervals). That keeps Anki parity and makes the casual path "left or right". Swipe: right = Good, left = Again, opt-in per interaction's proposal; up-Easy cut (hidden gesture for the rarest action, lesson #9). Add a **"Simple grading" setting** (Not yet / Got it, mapped to Again/Good), offered once in onboarding as a single question, not buried. Undo toast for both. Casual critic's "I'd never open settings" is why it is asked in onboarding. Strings 'Good — next review in.*' etc. stay as accessibility labels in both modes.

**3. Home, top to bottom:** header (avatar, greeting) -> **one Due card** (72pt row: "7 due · Start", or "All caught up · next in 10m" plus a soft capture nudge) -> sticker pile as a compact hero (not first, but visible without scrolling) -> search/filters -> grid. Serious wants Due first, casual wants stickers first; a single row costs the pile 72pt, which is cheap. Copy for the casual: "7 ready" not "7 due"; keep 'Start reviewing — 1 card due.*'. Duplicate lists (Latest finds, Latest stickers) go. I disagree with designer only if the Due card grows into a hero.

**4. Calendar: one screen, two modes already built.** Default Finds = photo diary (big sticker per day, "on this day" row). The existing Reviews toggle becomes the **forecast** (bars: due per day for 14 days, new/learning/review stacked). Retention %, intervals and heatmap go to a Stats page in Profile. Home's Due card carries today's count; Calendar does not need a second Review CTA (cut the duplicate).

**5. Navigation: no objection** to a labelled bar, modal Scan, avatar header. One requirement: the Collection tab shows a **due badge** (dot with count), because Home is no longer a pure queue.

**6. Study card: memory lives on the back, one tap away, never on the front.** I withdraw my round-1 note-under-the-photo on the front: it can leak the answer and adds reading. Front = photo, prompt, Hint, progress bar. Back = word, reading, meaning, sentence, then a single muted **Memory line** ("Café by the canal · 12 Nov · Paris") only when a note, place or date exists; "Not recorded" is hidden. Tap the thumbnail or "..." to open the full card detail with Where/When/edit/share/delete. That gives casual the memory and costs serious zero scroll.

**Single change that matters most: rebuild the study card and grade row as a quiet, ranked, one-glance flashcard (photo front, word-first back, primary Again/Good), because the review loop is the product and it is where "unfinished" shows hardest.**
