# Serious learner, round 2 rebuttal

**1. Colour.** I stay on Powder, and I'm moving on the learning expert's objection: peach near Again/Hard is real only if peach is used on grade buttons. It must not be. Peach is the single "go" action (Start, Add cards, Scan) and nothing in the grade row. Accessibility's own number (peach fill 1.98:1 on the page) means: navy ink #1B3359 plus a ring, no exceptions. On the blue Easy, I side against accessibility. Easy in blue on a blue-chrome Powder app makes the best answer look like a link or a selected tab, and I read the grade row by hue 100 times a day. Fix the deuteranopia collapse (Again vs Easy, ΔE 6) with luminance: Again dark and saturated, Easy light, plus a distinct border and the interval text. Keep green-ish Easy, shift it teal (~#2F7D6D) for CVD separation. Blue Easy would be tolerable on Rose, but that's an argument for not mixing.

**2. Grading.** I concede part of my round 1. The casual critic's "Got it / Not yet" is homework avoided, and I don't want two modes. One design: four buttons always visible, Again and Good the same large size, Hard and Easy smaller and visually secondary, each with its interval at 13px tabular. Swipe (left Again, right Good) is opt-in, off by default, per interaction. Undo is non-negotiable; I'd move on that alone. I reject a 2-button default: collapsing Hard/Easy ruins my interval data, and I'd leave.

**3. Home order.** Top to bottom: (a) one Due card, "N due · Start" with the time estimate, peach, full width; if zero, "All caught up, next in 3h", not an empty button. (b) Search plus filter chips. (c) The sticker grid. The casual critic wants the sticker pile first. I'd allow the pile only as the empty-state hero for a new account (it teaches the product), and as a small strip of "on this day" below Due. Kill "Latest finds" and "Latest stickers" as separate duplicate sections, and kill "GOAL 5" and "0 FOUND TODAY".

**4. Calendar.** One screen cannot honestly do both; it has been trying to, and the result is empty air (`05a-calendar-month.png`). Calendar = photo diary (casual's big sticker per day, Day screen, "on this day"). The forecast moves to Home as a one-line "Due: today 7, tomorrow 4, +3 days 11" and to a Stats screen in Profile with retention and forecast. The Finds/Reviews toggle goes away. I accept this because the diary is the product's differentiator; I don't need the calendar for reviews, only reliable numbers somewhere.

**5. Navigation.** No objection to a labelled full-width bar and raised Scan; the pill covering content is the worst defect in `ss4-collection.png`. Scan as a full-screen modal is right (also lets the consent screen stop floating over the tab bar). Avatar in a shared header is fine. Objection: Scan must not hide Collection's search or the Due card; keep the bar at ≤ 4 items.

**6. Study card.** Strongly with the learning expert: photo-led front, thin progress bar, close X and one "..." menu; no date, avatar, share or delete during review. Back: word, reading, meaning, sentence; grammar and word-by-word folded behind "More". To satisfy the casual critic without slowing me: the user's day-note and a small "Where / When" line sit in card detail and in the "..." menu ("Memory"), and the note appears once on the back as a single line only if the user wrote one. Never render "Not recorded". Mic row out of review. Keep the Maestro strings.

**Single change that matters most:** make the study loop a fast, quiet, one-screen flashcard (photo front, answer-first back, four graded buttons with undo) and strip every decorative or destructive control from it.
