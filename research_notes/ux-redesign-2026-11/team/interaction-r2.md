# Interaction, round 2

**1. Colour: Powder stands, with two concessions.** Learning is right that peach (#F2A27A) sits near Hard's amber and Again's red family. Fix: on the study screen peach never appears. Study chrome (progress, Reveal) uses navy #24406B; peach is the action colour on Home, Scan and sheets only. Accessibility is right that a peach fill is 1.98:1 on the page, so every peach button gets navy ink #1B3359 plus a 1pt navy-20% ring. **Blue Easy: rejected for Powder.** Blue is Powder's selected/link/info colour, so a blue Easy reads as "selected". It would be fine in Rose, but we're not in Rose. The colour-blind problem is real (Again/Easy merge at deuteranopia, dE 6). Fix with lightness and glyph, not hue: Again dark #B3363F with a retry glyph, Easy a light-ish teal-green with a check glyph, so the two differ by 25+ L points. Accessibility must re-run the CVD script on the final pair.

**2. Grading: one design serves both.** Four buttons stay (the serious critic, and the Maestro strings). Again and Good are solid 56pt. Hard and Easy are 56pt too (tap target) but tonal and lighter, so the eye finds the two real choices. This answers the casual critic's "I don't know what Hard 6m means": her two buttons are visibly Again and Good, and the other two are quiet. The interval is a 13pt second line. One-time coachmark on the first review: "Again = see it soon. Good = you knew it." No settings page. **I change my mind on swipe:** drop up = Easy. Swipe left = Again, right = Good only (agrees with Learning), with undo as a 5s snackbar. Buttons are always visible.

**3. Home, top to bottom.** (a) Large title + avatar. (b) One Due card: "3 due · ~2 min · Start", or "All caught up, next in 3h" at zero. Not duplicated anywhere else. (c) One "From your finds" row: a single horizontally scrolling strip of recent or "on this day" stickers, tap = open card. That's the casual critic's reason to open the app and the serious critic's cheap discard-free row. (d) Filter/sort, then the grid. The pink hero wall and "Latest stickers" leave Home; the wall moves to Boards. An empty account gets a Scan CTA in slot (b). Designer and Learning are right; the casual critic gets (c), not a hero.

**4. Calendar: one screen, two modes, and the segmented control is the mechanism.** Finds = photo diary, big sticker per day, tap opens that day. Reviews = a 14-day forecast strip over a heat-tint month. The duplicate week chart goes. "On this day" is Home's strip, not Calendar's. Retention % goes to Profile, because a stat nobody acts on shouldn't occupy a tab. Critic-casual and Learning each get their half.

**5. Navigation: no objection.** Two conditions: (i) Scan modal must keep the camera session pre-warmed, or the 380ms slide shows a black frame; (ii) Profile as formSheet needs a device check, with pushed-card as the fallback. Labels on, 11pt, per accessibility.

**6. Study card: memory lives after the answer, never before it.** Front is photo-led with only X, progress bar and the prompt. No date, avatar, share or delete. On the back, a single 13pt line beneath the content, "Shot 12 Nov · Kitchen", hidden when the data is "Not recorded". Tapping it opens card detail as a sheet over the session (session state preserved). Share/download/delete exist only in detail, delete in a destructive action sheet. The memory costs zero seconds because it's visible only once you've already answered. Order word, reading, meaning, sentence, grammar folded (Learning, agreed). Engineer: Maestro strings stay in the accessibility labels.

**Single change that matters most:** one shared screen header and one modal vocabulary used on every screen, so the app stops being five hand-built apps.
