# Round 2: rebuttal

All seven round-1 positions are in `team/`, along with `team/moderator-notes.md`.
Read every one of them before writing. Then write `team/<your-role>-r2.md` (≤450 words) taking a clear position on
each disputed point below. You may change your mind, and saying so is valuable.
Argue against specific claims by naming the role. No restating your round 1.

1. **Colour: Rose or Powder.** Votes: designer, interaction, both critics →
   Powder; learning → Rose (peach action too close to the Again/Hard hues);
   accessibility → Rose passes light mode with fewer fixes, but Powder is
   better in dark (peach 8.8:1 on navy) and its peach fill is only 1.98:1 on
   the page, so it needs navy ink #1B3359 and a ring. Accessibility also
   proposes a *blue* Easy (#2B6CB0) for colour-blind safety. Does a blue Easy
   clash with Powder's blue chrome, or with Rose? Settle it.
2. **Grading: 4 buttons vs 2.** Serious → 4 with intervals. Casual → 2.
   Interaction → opt-in swipe (left Again, right Good, up Easy) + undo.
   Is there one design that serves both (e.g. 4 buttons, with Hard/Easy
   visually secondary, plus swipe)?
3. **What Home leads with.** Serious/learning → "N due · Start", with no
   sticker-wall hero. Casual → the stickers and memories are why they open the
   app. Designer → one Due card, duplicates cut. Decide the order of Home
   from top to bottom.
4. **Calendar's job.** Learning → review forecast first. Casual → a photo
   diary with a big sticker per day plus "on this day". Can one screen do both,
   or should the forecast move elsewhere (Home, stats in Profile)?
5. **Navigation.** Labelled full-width tab bar + raised Scan (designer,
   interaction) vs keeping the pill; Scan as a full-screen modal; Profile
   via an avatar in a shared header. Any objection?
6. **The study card.** Learning → photo-led front, bare progress bar, no
   date/avatar/share/delete during review; back ordered word → reading →
   meaning → sentence, grammar folded. Casual → wants the memory (photo,
   when/where) present. Where do memory details live without slowing review?

End with **one line: the single change that matters most** for making the
app feel finished.
