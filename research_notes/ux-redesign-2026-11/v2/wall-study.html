The sticker wall, rebuilt

  • Gone: tape strips, ruled paper, the sparkle "new" badge, the floating Arrange pill, the breathing animation.

  • Composition, not scatter: until you arrange it yourself, your newest sticker is the hero, two flank it and up to three sit behind. It holds up from one sticker to six.
