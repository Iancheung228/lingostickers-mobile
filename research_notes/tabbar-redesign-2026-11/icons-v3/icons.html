Rationale

  • Collection. Two straight, closed landscape flash cards, the back one offset up-left, with two word lines on the front card. The back card stays an outline when the front fills. The top-right 7x7 badge zone is ink-free apart from a sliver under 0.4 unit. Rejected after in-bar review: tilted back card (reads as a handle or strap), narrower stacked edges (handbag), portrait offset deck (too close to the system copy glyph), crossed cards (backpack).
  • Calendar. Squircle body, two pegs, header rule and a round "today" seed. Scaled to 94% so its ink matches the others.
  • Boards. Uneven masonry: four squircle tiles in two columns of different heights, with one small round sticker inset in the top-left tile. No pill and no circle outline, so it cannot read as the digits "08"; the 3.2 unit gaps keep tiles separate; filled is the identical geometry, filled. The even 2x2 option was rejected as the generic apps grid; the banner-plus-two option as a widget.
  • Profile. Designer-widened closed bust and head (head r 4.3, bust 3.6..20.4). Kept as is.
  • Camera. Same corner logic as the tabs (squircle-tightened, body radius 4.5), a crest swell on top, a lens whose ring is exactly the stroke width (1.65) around a seed, and one flash dot. One evenodd path, so the disc colour shows through.

Shared traits. One corner radius scale (4.25 / 3.4 / 2.6) with squircle-tightened corners; round caps and joins; one stroke (1.65); and a "seed", a small round form that is the focal point (today dot, inset sticker, head, lens). Deliberately quiet.

Grid and optical sizes

24x24, live area 20x20 (2 unit margin). Keylines are drawn on the stroke's outer edge: circle d20, square 18, portrait 16x20, landscape 20x16. Boards is a square-keyline glyph (18x18 outer), Calendar a square with pegs poking above, Collection a portrait card with a deck edge, Profile a portrait. Nothing is circle-keyline, so no glyph needs the d20 overshoot. Outer bounding boxes are centred within 0.2 units of (12,12) (measured by rasterising at 16x). Ink area of the outlines: Collection 135, Calendar 135, Boards 139, Profile 99.

Stroke weight test

Tested 1.5, 1.65 and 1.75 beside 11 pt Newsreader 500 at 1x and 3x. 1.5 goes spindly beside the serif's stems and the knocked-out details in the filled states thin out. 1.75 closes the 1.55-unit gaps in Boards and the deck. 1.65 (1.72 pt at 25 pt, about 5 px at @3x) holds both.

Final at 1x and 3x

Explorations

Three per glyph, outline and filled. Chosen ones are outlined. Rejected, with reasons: dot-and-line card interiors read as faces; a single tilted back card read as a padlock, then a mouse with a cable, and then a backpack in the filled state; a three-deep stack was too dense; the frame-with-stickers Boards looked like a photo icon; the avatar-ring Profile is too heavy when filled and has a different keyline than its neighbours.

Self-critique, open issues

  • Collection is still two overlapping cards; the tilt, word lines and surviving back outline are what separate it from the system copy glyph. It is the weakest on distinctiveness. It is still two offset cards, so a copy reading is possible at a glance; landscape proportion and word lines are the differentiators.
  • Boards is the heaviest outline (ink 154 vs 130 to 135); 1.75 would worsen that, so 1.65 stays. It could read as an apps grid at a glance; the inset sticker and uneven heights are the differentiators.
  • The family trait (the seed) is subtle by design; if the owner wants more ownable character, the next step is custom label-matched details, not a bigger motif.
