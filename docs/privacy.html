Tabi Stickers Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Tabi Stickers ("we," "us," "the app") is a language-learning app that turns photos of real objects into vocabulary stickers. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and password (handled by our backend provider, Supabase — we never see or store your raw password ourselves).

Photos. When you take or choose a photo to turn into a sticker, that image is uploaded and processed to create your sticker. Photos you capture are stored so your sticker collection persists across sessions.

Voice recordings. If you choose to record yourself practicing a word's pronunciation, that audio is captured via your device's microphone. This only happens when you actively start a recording — the app does not record in the background.

Location (optional). If you choose to tag a sticker with where you made it, we store that location alongside the sticker. This is entirely optional and off by default — the app never accesses your location unless you tap to add it to a specific sticker.

Push notification token. If you enable notifications, we store a device push token (issued by Apple/Google via Expo's push service) so we can deliver challenge and friend-activity notifications to you.

Friends & social data. If you use the friends/challenge features, we store the connections you create (friend requests, challenge results) so those features work.

How We Use This Information

  • To create and store your sticker collection
  • To generate vocabulary text and definitions for your stickers
  • To remove image backgrounds so stickers look clean
  • To let you optionally map where a sticker was made
  • To send you notifications about friend challenges, if enabled
  • To operate account features like sign-in and password reset

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your photos, recordings, or location data for advertising.

Data Storage & Retention

Your data is stored for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account, which permanently removes your profile, stickers, photos, and account data from our systems.

Your Choices

  • Location is opt-in per sticker — you decide each time.
  • Notifications can be disabled anytime in your device's system settings.
  • Account deletion is available in-app (Settings → Delete Account) and is permanent.

Children's Privacy

Tabi Stickers is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
