Tabi Stickers Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Tabi Stickers ("we," "us," "the app") is a language-learning app that turns photos of real objects into vocabulary stickers and flashcards. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who processes it, and how to delete it.

Information We Collect

Account information. Your email address and password (handled by our backend provider, Supabase — we never see your raw password), a username, an optional profile picture, and your chosen learning language.

Photos. When you scan an object, choose a photo from your library, or add an optional “memory” photo to a sticker, the image is uploaded to your private storage so your collection persists. Where possible the cutout is made on your device (using Apple's Vision framework). Before your first scan the app asks for your explicit permission to send the photo to the third-party services listed below. Your profile picture, if you set one, is stored in a publicly readable storage location; anyone who has its link can view it.

Cards and study data. The words, translations, sentences, notes and edits you create or type, plus your review history and schedule.

Voice recordings. If you choose to record yourself practicing a word's pronunciation, the audio is captured via your microphone only while you record, and is saved to your private storage as a voice note attached to that sticker. Recordings are not sent to the AI services below.

Location (optional). If you tap to tag a sticker with where you made it, we store the coordinates (latitude and longitude) and a place name alongside the sticker. The place name is looked up from the coordinates using Apple's on-device geocoding service, which may send the coordinates to Apple. If you import a photo from your library that already contains GPS data, the app may read that location from the photo to tag the sticker the same way. Location is never collected in the background.

Push notification token. If you enable notifications, we store a device push token (issued by Apple via Expo's push service) so we can deliver notifications to you. Notifications are used only by the friends/challenge features, which are not enabled in this version of the app.

Friends & social data. Friends, challenges, blocking and reporting are not enabled in this version of the app. If a later version enables them, we will store the connections you create (friend requests, challenge results, blocks and reports) so those features work, and this policy will be updated first.

Usage analytics. We use Aptabase, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count anonymous events such as app launches, a sticker being saved or shared, and whether a cutout was made on-device or needed a fallback service (with technical measurements of the cutout and, on failure, an error message). We do not attach your account, email or username to these events. Aptabase receives basic device context such as app version, OS version, device model and locale. We do not use advertising identifiers and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Usage counters. To limit how many AI-assisted scans each account can make per day, our servers keep a per-account daily counter.

We do not use a crash-reporting service.

How We Use This Information

  • To create and store your sticker collection and review schedule
  • To generate vocabulary text and definitions for your stickers
  • To remove image backgrounds so stickers look clean
  • To let you optionally map where a sticker was made
  • To operate account features like sign-in and password reset
  • To understand, in aggregate, whether the app works (anonymous analytics)

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your photos, recordings, or location data for advertising.

Sharing With Other Users

In this version of the app, nothing you create is shared with other users, with one exception: your profile picture, if you set one, is publicly readable by link. Social features (friends, challenges, blocking and reporting) are not enabled. If they are enabled in a future version, adding a friend will not give them access to your collection, and sending a challenge would share a copy of that sticker's word, translation, sentence and image with the friend you send it to.

Data Storage & Retention

Your data is stored for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account. Deletion removes your account, profile, stickers, cards, review history, and the files in your private photo and voice-note storage and your profile-picture storage.

We do not currently keep database backups, so data you delete is not kept in a backup copy. If we start keeping backups, deleted data could remain in them for up to 30 days before they expire, and this policy will say so first. Our hosting provider keeps routine server logs (which can include your account ID and IP address) for at most 7 days.

Reports of abusive content, a feature not enabled in this version, are kept so repeated abuse can be acted on. When you delete your account, reports you filed are deleted; a report about you is kept, but no longer linked to your account.

Anonymous analytics events are held by Aptabase under its own retention policy and are not linked to your account, so they are not removed when you delete your account.

Your Choices

  • Location is opt-in per sticker, and your device asks for permission first.
  • Microphone, camera and photos are used only after you grant permission, which you can withdraw in your device's Settings.
  • Notifications can be disabled anytime in your device's system settings.
  • Sending photos to AI services requires your permission before your first scan; without it, scanning is not available.
  • Account deletion is available in-app (Profile → Delete Account) and is permanent.

Children's Privacy

Tabi Stickers is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your information, including encrypted connections and per-user access controls, so that only you can access your own account data (other than your public profile picture).

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
