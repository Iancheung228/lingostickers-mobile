Tabi Stickers

Point your camera at something. Keep the word.

What it does

  1. Scan an object — your phone cuts it out of the photo, right there on the device.
  2. Get the word — in the language you're learning, with a reading and a sentence about the actual scene you photographed.
  3. Keep it — every find becomes a sticker you can pin to a board, revisit on a calendar, or send to a friend as a challenge.

It's a keepsake more than a lesson. The stickers are of your kitchen, your walk, your trip — so the word comes back attached to the moment you found it.
